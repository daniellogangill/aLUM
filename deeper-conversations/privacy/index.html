Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Design My Thinking ("Design My Thinking", "we", "us" or "our") handles personal information in connection with Deeper Conversations.

In plain English: we try to collect as little as practical, we use data to provide your private workspace and AI-assisted drafting, we do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your content to train our own general-purpose AI model. Deeper Conversations does not automatically publish content to LinkedIn®.

1. Who operates Deeper Conversations

Deeper Conversations is a development-stage product operated by Design My Thinking in Australia. It is currently provided on a private, invitation-only basis.

Privacy questions, requests or complaints can be sent to hello@designmythinking.xyz.

2. Information we may collect

The information we handle depends on which parts of the service you use.

Account and access information

  • email address and authentication information needed to sign you in;
  • basic account identifiers and timestamps;
  • display name or timezone if supplied.

Workspace preferences

  • topics, themes, keywords and relevance preferences;
  • posting or engagement cadence preferences;
  • voice and writing preferences, including preferred and avoided writing patterns;
  • in-progress workspace state used to restore your session.

Content you provide

  • professional posts, links, screenshots or text you explicitly provide for analysis;
  • voice samples or other writing examples you choose to provide;
  • drafts, edited drafts, final text, notes and interaction history;
  • names, organisations, profile links and relationship context about professional contacts where you choose to provide them.

Optional LinkedIn archive imports

You may choose to import selected CSV files from your own LinkedIn account archive. The importer may use information from files such as Connections, Comments, Reactions, Shares, Posts and Articles to build your private People, history and Voice DNA records. Depending on the file, this may include names, organisation, position, profile URL, connection dates, post or comment text, reaction types, dates and links.

Private LinkedIn message export files are deliberately ignored by the current importer. You do not need to provide a LinkedIn archive to use the service.

Optional notification signals

If you choose to enable a user-controlled notification ingestion workflow, Deeper Conversations may receive normalised information derived from LinkedIn notification emails, such as the actor or author name, profile or post URL, visible snippet, signal type, receipt time and limited email metadata. The current workflow is designed not to store the raw email body.

External content

The service may also collect or cache non-personal public information from external sources such as article titles, links, summaries, publisher details and publication dates so that it can surface professional conversations that may be relevant to your configured themes.

3. How we collect information

We collect information when you create or use an account, configure your workspace, type or upload content, import an archive, use an optional notification workflow, or otherwise interact with the service. Some information is generated by the service itself, such as scores, recommendations, AI-generated drafts and usage metadata.

If an authorised LinkedIn integration is introduced, information may also be obtained through official LinkedIn APIs after the user has provided any consent required for that integration.

4. Why we use information

We use information only for purposes reasonably connected with operating and improving Deeper Conversations, including to:

  • authenticate users and maintain private workspaces;
  • remember themes, preferences, voice settings and in-progress work;
  • find, rank and explain potentially relevant professional conversations;
  • analyse user-supplied professional content;
  • develop points of view and generate draft comments or posts;
  • maintain user-requested history, people and relationship context;
  • protect the service, diagnose errors and prevent misuse;
  • respond to support, privacy or deletion requests;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations and platform requirements.

5. AI processing

Deeper Conversations uses artificial intelligence to assist with analysis and drafting. Selected information may be sent to the OpenAI API when an AI feature is used. Depending on the feature, this may include a user-supplied post or screenshot, source summaries, configured themes, a proposed point of view, writing preferences and limited professional relationship context.

AI outputs can be incomplete, inaccurate or inappropriate. They are suggestions only. Deeper Conversations is designed around human review: the user decides whether to edit, use or discard a draft and is responsible for anything they ultimately publish.

Design My Thinking does not use user content to train its own general-purpose AI model. As at the date of this policy, OpenAI states that inputs and outputs submitted through its API are not used to train its models by default unless the API customer explicitly opts in to data sharing. Design My Thinking does not intend to opt user traffic into model training without first updating our disclosures and, where appropriate, seeking consent.

OpenAI may retain API inputs and outputs for a limited period for service operation, security and abuse monitoring in accordance with its own policies. Current standard API documentation states that this may be up to 30 days for many API uses, subject to applicable product settings, legal requirements and changes to OpenAI's services.

7. LinkedIn information

Deeper Conversations is an independent product and is not endorsed by or affiliated with LinkedIn Corporation.

Where a user voluntarily uploads information from their own LinkedIn archive, that information is treated as user-provided content and handled under this policy.

If we introduce an official LinkedIn API integration, information obtained through that API will also be handled in accordance with the applicable LinkedIn API terms, permissions and retention restrictions. This may include stronger or shorter deletion requirements than apply to other data.

We do not use the service to scrape LinkedIn, and Deeper Conversations does not automatically publish content to LinkedIn.

8. Information about other people

Professional content can contain information about other people. You should only provide personal information or content that you are entitled to use for the purpose for which you provide it. Do not provide sensitive, confidential or private information about another person unless you have a lawful basis and appropriate authority to do so.

Deeper Conversations is not intended to infer sensitive characteristics about other people from professional content.

9. Storage, security and browser data

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps appropriate to the development-stage service to reduce the risk of unauthorised access, misuse, loss or disclosure. Current controls include authenticated access, user-scoped database records and row-level access controls for private application tables.

The service may use essential browser storage, cookies or similar technologies for authentication, session management and restoring in-progress work. We do not currently use advertising cookies or behavioural advertising trackers in Deeper Conversations.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Users should avoid entering information that is not reasonably necessary for the professional-conversation and drafting purposes of the service.

10. Retention and deletion

We generally retain account and workspace information for as long as the account remains active or as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, meet security needs and comply with legal obligations.

You may request deletion by contacting hello@designmythinking.xyz. For general account data, we aim to remove user-linked information from active systems within 30 days of a valid deletion request or account closure, except where limited retention is reasonably required for backups, security records, dispute resolution or legal obligations.

LinkedIn API data is different: if information has been collected through an official LinkedIn API, we will delete that information, access tokens and related LinkedIn API content as promptly as required by the applicable LinkedIn terms, including immediately where those terms require it.

Information may remain for a limited time in backups or provider systems where immediate deletion is technically impracticable, provided it is protected from ordinary use and removed in the normal deletion cycle.

11. Access and correction

You may ask what personal information we hold about you and request correction of information that is inaccurate, out of date or incomplete. Contact hello@designmythinking.xyz. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

12. Children

Deeper Conversations is intended for professional use and is not directed to children. If broader access is introduced, the intended minimum age will be 18 unless we expressly state otherwise.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product, providers or legal requirements change. We will update the date at the top of the policy and, for material changes affecting active users, take reasonable steps to make the change visible.

14. Questions and complaints

If you have a privacy question, deletion request or complaint, contact Design My Thinking at hello@designmythinking.xyz. We will aim to respond within a reasonable period and work with you to address the issue.
