A Design My Thinking experiment

Deeper Conversations.
Less manufactured engagement.

A private, human-in-the-loop tool for finding professional conversations worth entering, developing a point of view, and turning that thinking into a draft that still sounds like you.

Open private app → How data & AI work

Development access is currently private and invitation-only.

Built around a simple boundary

Think with AI

AI can help interpret a conversation, pressure-test a point of view and produce a starting draft.

Keep a human in the loop

The user reviews, edits and decides what to say. The product does not automatically publish to LinkedIn®.

Collect less

Account information is deliberately light. Optional LinkedIn archive imports are user-controlled, and private message exports are ignored by design.

Private by design, transparent by default

The product stores only the information needed to maintain a private workspace and improve the usefulness of your drafts. Selected information is processed by service providers such as Supabase, Vercel and OpenAI. Our legal and plain-English pages explain what is collected, where it goes and how deletion works.
